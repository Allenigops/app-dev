##  About This Page

Spider-Man is my favorite movie. This page is about why it means so much to me, and how Peter Parker's story feels a lot like my own.

> *"With great power comes great responsibility."*

---

##  Where Our Stories Meet

I lost my parents at a very young age. Peter Parker lost his parents too, and later lost Uncle Ben, the person who raised him. When I watch him, I don't just see a superhero. I see someone who knows what it feels like to lose the people he loves.

### What We Have in Common

- Losing our parents when we were young
- Growing up with the support of other people who cared for us
- Learning to be strong even when things were hard
- Wanting to make the people we lost proud

---

## 📊 Peter's Life vs. My Life

| | Peter Parker | Me |
|---|---|---|
| **Early loss** | Lost his parents as a child | Lost my parents at a very young age |
| **Who helped** | Uncle Ben and Aunt May | The people and community who supported me |
| **Challenge** | Balancing school and responsibility | Balancing studies and life |
| **What keeps going** | The promise to do good | The goal of building a better future |

---

##  What I Learned From Spider-Man

1. **Loss does not define you.** What you do next does.
2. **Responsibility is a choice.** Peter keeps choosing to help others.
3. **Kindness matters.** Small acts of help can change someone's life.
4. **You are never fully alone.** People who care show up in different ways.
